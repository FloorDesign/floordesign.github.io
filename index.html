FloorDesign

Interior design application for macOS. Effortlessly create 2D home plans, customize room colors and textures, and drag furniture, doors, windows from an organized catalog.

Customizable Rooms

Drag to create rooms, double-click to edit walls, intersections, and measurements. Precise control over every detail.

Reusable Templates

Right-click any room to add to your template list. Reuse designs across projects with one click.

Pre-Defined Objects

Thousands of ready-to-use elements: doors, windows, stairs, chairs, sofas. Organized by category for instant access.

Extensive Symbol Library

Over 3,000 beautiful symbols, plus basic shapes like text boxes, lines, and callouts. Drag images directly from Finder or paste from clipboard.

What's New in 3.2.9

Full support for macOS 26 Tahoe. Refined UI and bug fixes for smoother performance.

How It Works

Step 1: Build Your Floor Plan

Drag room objects into the canvas. Double-click to customize dimensions, walls, and intersections.

Step 2: Add Elements

Browse the catalog for furniture, doors, windows. Drag and drop anywhere on your plan.

Step 3: Customize and Refine

Change colors, textures, or paste images from clipboard. Use symbols and shapes for details.

Step 4: Save and Reuse

Add rooms to templates. Export or iterate on your design anytime.

FAQ

Is FloorDesign compatible with the latest macOS?

Yes, version 3.2.9 fully supports macOS 26 Tahoe and earlier versions.

Do I need to purchase anything?

No, this is the fully activated version of FloorDesign. Ready to use immediately.

Can I import my own images?

Yes, drag files from Finder or paste from clipboard (Cmd+V) directly into your design.

How do I reuse room designs?

Right-click a room and select "Add to template list" for quick access in future projects.
